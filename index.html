The science behind colour change

Anthocyanin pH sensor

Clitoria ternatea flower extract contains anthocyanins. In neutral pH they remain blue; in acidic oral environment (pH < 5.5) they convert to flavylium cation → purple/pink.

Optimized stability: Trisodium citrate buffers paste at pH 6-7, and reduced SLS prevents pigment degradation.

Remedial action

Neem, tulsi, xylitol, and sodium bicarbonate create an unfavorable environment for cariogenic bacteria (S. mutans). Tannic acid provides astringent & anti‑inflammatory effect.

Reference: Shafer’s Oral Pathology, Essentials of Preventive Dentistry.
